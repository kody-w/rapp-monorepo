# Governance & Escalation — Project Phoenix

## Escalation path (blockers)

1. **Team member → Workstream Lead** — raise within the workstream first (see Workstream Directory).
2. **Workstream Lead → PMO Lead (Oliver Brandt)** — any blocker open **more than 3 working days**, or any blocker that crosses workstreams, must be escalated to the PMO.
3. **PMO Lead → Program Director (Katrin Vogel)** — blockers with budget impact >€50k, timeline impact >1 week, or unresolved after PMO mediation.
4. **Program Director → Steering Committee** — chaired by **Henrik Larsen (CFO)**; meets monthly, extraordinary sessions on 48h notice for go/no-go decisions.

If the person at any step is unavailable, escalate to their named **backup** in the Workstream
Directory; backups hold full decision authority for up to two weeks.

## Risk & decision registers

Maintained by the PMO in the program site: every escalation gets a register ID (RSK-#### / DEC-####)
and a named owner. Decisions are binding once minuted by the PMO.

## RACI summary

- **Workstream Leads**: Accountable for design + delivery inside their stream.
- **PMO (Oliver Brandt)**: Responsible for plan integrity, dependencies, escalation brokering.
- **Program Director (Katrin Vogel)**: Accountable for the program; tie-breaker below Steering.
- **Steering Committee**: Ultimate decision body.
