### Sandbox client 210 provisioning and exercises

Hands-on practice in sandbox client 210 is constrained by availability rather than by content, which is limiting the exercise hours key users can book. Sven Andersson is introducing a booking system and publishing refresh windows in advance so a session is never lost to an unannounced refresh. Sofia Rossi asked for exercise completion to be reported alongside curriculum completion from 2 May 2026, since the 90% pass gate applies to the exercises.

**Status:** Green · **Owner:** Sofia Rossi · **Next checkpoint:** 27 April 2026

### Site readiness and floor-walker planning

Floor-walker coverage is planned at roughly one per twenty users for week one, which is what determines the ticket volume reaching the war room. Amara Nwosu raised that the same people are being proposed as floor-walkers and as hypercare support, which would leave both thin. The two roles are being separated, with floor-walkers drawn primarily from the champions network, and Camila Reyes will publish the split by 30 May 2026.

**Status:** Amber · **Owner:** Amara Nwosu · **Next checkpoint:** 5 May 2026
