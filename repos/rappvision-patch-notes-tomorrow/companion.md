# Paired publication companion

## WHAT IT IS

A 26.8-second fictional 2036 bulletin paired with a deterministic branching newsroom replay. Both begin with Civic OS 12 making every public queue visible.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The film teaches the premise first. The replay then hands control to the viewer, demonstrating how one invented event can produce two incompatible futures without presenting either as fact.

## LOCATION

- Guided film: `media/civic-os-12.mp4` or `media/civic-os-12.webm`
- Live replay: `live/index.html`
- Paired manifest: `channel.json`

## EVIDENCE

1. Run **Outcome A — Coordinate**.
2. Run **Outcome B — Forecast**.
3. Select **Compare outcomes**.
4. Select **Reset** and confirm both panels return to “Not run.”

`evidence.json` records encode probes, distinct SHA-256 hashes, disclosure checks, replay behavior, and privacy validation.

## BOUNDARIES

Kira Voss and Tomorrow Public Service are fictional. No real person, outlet, product, event, or forecast is represented. All claims and metrics are invented. The replay uses no external data, analytics, or persistent storage.

## KNOWN COMPROMISES

The synthetic correspondent is represented by abstract geometry rather than a human likeness. The replay is intentionally compact and models two authored branches rather than an open-ended forecast.

## NEXT DECISION

Choose whether to watch the guided bulletin again or use the replay to test the opposite branch order. Reset always restores the same baseline.

## Accessibility

The film carries persistent visual disclosure and readable lower thirds. The replay uses native buttons, keyboard focus, a status region, high contrast, and responsive layout.

## Provenance

Visuals and interaction were authored for this pilot. Narration is locally synthesized. No external footage, copyrighted music, private data, or network calls are used.
