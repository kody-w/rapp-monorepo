Positive acceleration: the already-sliding block speeds up to the right.

Ffriction = μ × m × g = 0.25 × 2.00 × 9.8 = 4.90 N
a = (Fapplied − Ffriction) ÷ m = (10.0 − 4.90) ÷ 2.00 = 2.55 m/s²
Units and scope. N means newtons, kg means kilograms, and m/s² means velocity changes by that many metres per second each second. The equation is a = (F − μmg) / m. It models a block already sliding right on a flat horizontal table, while the applied force remains constant. Negative acceleration means the block slows while it is still sliding; the model stops at rest because static friction is outside this replay. Air drag, rotation, and changing surfaces are also out of scope.
