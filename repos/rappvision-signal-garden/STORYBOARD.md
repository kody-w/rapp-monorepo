## Video direction

Cream field-journal paper carries every frame. Chlorophyll green is the stable
signal, rust is the anomaly, and indigo ink holds labels. Motion uses smooth
long-tail settles and voice-paced reveals: no element appears before its spoken
cue. The garden is one continuous stage, with a pulse line, threshold ruler,
specimen iris, and exact readout sharing visual space. Frame 3 deliberately
holds the threshold crossing for comprehension. Never use a dashboard grid,
neon technology styling, a front-loaded slideshow, or independent floating
screensaver motion.
