Canonical `rapp-work/1` gives an organization exactly one accountable owner, so a group of equals has no natural fit. One idea, not yet proposed to `rapp-work/1`, makes the owner the Hive's notary: they sign at the edge only what the Hive already approved, and name that approval. No specification provides notarizing today.
