SDK migration refuses a Hive that was seeded from a repository, and refuses world ids over 64 characters. That cap lives in `rapp-hive/1`'s `schema.json` (`$defs/label`) and in the SDK's `migration.py`. The proposed fix uses pointer-only successors and allows world ids up to 128 characters.
