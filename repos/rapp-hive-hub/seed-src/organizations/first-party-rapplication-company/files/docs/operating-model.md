# Operating model

This is a configurable company pattern, not an activated organization. The
owner chooses its purpose, persona label, trusted tools, and authorized people
or agents. Seven workspaces do not mean seven permanent processes.

| Workspace | Owns | Does not own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ceo-office | Focus, mission, craftsmanship critique, recommendation | Independent acceptance, unilateral promotion, owner merge |
| product | Scope, exclusions, acceptance, iteration priorities | Invented demand or user research |
| design | Conversation, language, recoverable states, access needs | Claims of unobserved accessibility |
| engineering | Implementation, deterministic checks, exact candidate | Verification of its own work as independent |
| quality | Independent reproduction, live-use observations, public-safety review | Building the candidate it independently approves |
| release | Authorized internal packaging, public projection, PR handoff | Granting its own authority or merging for the owner |
| support | Minimal reproduction, consent-aware feedback, help | Collection of private conversations by default |
| casework | Shared intake, declared inputs, accepted output references | Membership or signing authority |

The native `rapp-work-sdk/1` Organization registers only pointers to same-world
team and casework Workspaces. It must not absorb their content, reference
foreign-world members as local, copy identity stores, or introduce another
membership or approval ledger. The exact SDK and RAPP/1 pins come from the
package, not a floating branch.

## Configure without impersonating

The owner reviews `templates/founder-ceo.json` and
`templates/ceo-charter.md`. Select an original persona or a neutral role label;
never claim to be a real person. Style is presentation, not identity or power.
Fill real role bindings only in the consumer's private workspace, referencing
the host's existing validated identities and grants. Unfilled slots block work
that needs them.

Builders, verifiers, and the external decider must be checked by identity, not
by department names. A verifier is distinct from every builder and the CEO.
The external decider is also distinct from builders and the CEO and issues a
separate decision after verification. Even if an owner permits a verifier to
be the external decider, one record cannot fulfill both gates.

Start with one candidate in progress. Assign bounded, non-overlapping tasks
with explicit inputs, outputs, acceptance, and effect approval. Work can be
done sequentially by the owner's host; the seed starts no agent loop.

## Evidence and storage

Keep source tests, real chat observations, structural checks, authenticated
acceptance, recommendations, decisions, and publication facts separate.
Use exact artifact hashes and existing native record references where available.
Do not turn a filled form, screenshot, or passing fixture into a signed record.
Keep failed observations and create linked successors rather than editing history.

Only a separately selected public distribution may cross into a public store.
No private organization root, workspace dump, native identity, private locator,
raw transcript, or receipt store belongs in it. Sanitized evidence summaries
may be proposed only after explicit review and publication approval.
