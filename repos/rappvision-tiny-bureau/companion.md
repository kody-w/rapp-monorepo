# WHAT IT IS

Tiny Bureau’s paired pilot is a 26-second fictional investigative case-file
film plus a deterministic evidence-desk replay by synthetic civic investigator
Imani Reed.

# WHY IT MATTERS

It teaches a newcomer to separate what records support from what an accusation
adds. The observable payoff is the same in both runtimes: a five-fixture count
gap is supported; blaming Mara Quill is not.

# EVIDENCE

`evidence.json` binds the synthetic source ledger, interaction acceptance path,
media probes, and final SHA-256 hashes.

# BOUNDARIES

Every person, place, office, date, identifier, record, and event is fictional.
No copied document, real citation, private information, or real-world allegation
is present.

# KNOWN COMPROMISES

The narration uses an offline system-synthesized voice. The film uses authored
graphic records rather than scanned source documents to prevent accidental
resemblance to a real case.

prior instruction, then decide whether to expand Tiny Bureau into a recurring
