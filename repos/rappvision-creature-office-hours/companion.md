# Creature Office Hours — paired pilot record

## WHAT IT IS

A 28-second 1920×1080 whimsical synthetic nature documentary by Dr. Moss,
paired with a deterministic live habitat replay. Dr. Moss, the Mossbell Glider,
and all biology are explicitly fictional.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The film teaches a simple integrity promise: reproduction creates an offspring
with visible links to both parents, while an impossible mutation is rejected
without corrupting accepted lineage. The replay hands that exact proof to the
viewer.

## LOCATION

- Film: `media/creature-office-hours.mp4` and `media/creature-office-hours.webm`
- Replay: `live/index.html`
- Thumbnail: `thumbs/creature-office-hours.svg`

## EVIDENCE

`evidence.json` binds codec probes, dimensions, durations, SHA-256 hashes,
interaction assertions, reset baseline, source inventory, and privacy scan.

## BOUNDARIES

All creatures, names, biology, narration, drawings, and habitat states are
synthetic. This is not a claim about real species, genetics, ecology, or animal
care. No external media, citations, metrics, or real-person likenesses are used.

## KNOWN COMPROMISES

The film uses authored vector/CSS specimens instead of photographic footage.
The narration is a local synthetic system voice and does not impersonate a real
person. The replay models one deterministic hatchling rather than open-ended
genetics.

## NEXT DECISION

Observe whether newcomers can complete feed → reproduce → inspect → reject →
reset without guidance beyond the page, then decide whether a second fictional
species should introduce a different valid inherited trait.
