`quarantined-candidate` status document that consumers must refuse as
authenticated registry evidence. Its current SHA-256 is
`736a9ae026b4be0d602a1a405195115ef1ab15484b5748e1502664b349f41e2d`.

own explicit dispositions. For the retired mirror assertion, see
