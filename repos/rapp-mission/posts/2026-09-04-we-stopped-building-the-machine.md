# We stopped building the machine

*2026-09-04*

For a long time the natural thing to build was the machine: the runtime, the chat window, the
harness that holds an AI and makes it do work. We built one. It works. It still exists.

Then the platforms got good. Claude Code, Copilot, and the ones coming after them ship a better
machine every month than a small team can. Competing with that is not brave. It is a way to
spend a year adding a second system beside the one people already have, and calling the extra
weight a product.

So we moved our effort to the one place the platforms cannot go for you: the file that makes
their AI yours. It rides on top of whatever is native. It makes that AI know you and do more. It
never shows itself. When you switch tools, it comes along, and the next AI is just as good at
helping you as the last one, because the same file is underneath.

Our own runtime is now the last-case scenario. If every native platform fails, it can run the
same file with no platform, no network, and no account. That is all it is for. Most people will
never know it exists, and that is the point.

We measure ourselves by how much better the AI in front of you gets, not by anything of ours you
can see.
