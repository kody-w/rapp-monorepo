# Rapp Hive Privacy Policy

_Last updated September 25, 2026_

Rapp Hive does not collect personal data. It talks only to the RAPP Brainstem you point it at, and its developer never receives your conversations, voice, agents, or settings.

## What the app stores on your device

Your brainstem’s address and whether replies are spoken aloud, in the app’s private on-device storage. On the web, these are saved in your browser’s local storage for this site.

Your brainstem’s secret, only if you enter one. On iPhone and Android it is kept in the device keychain, limited to this device, and sent only to the address it was saved for; changing the address forgets it. On the web it is kept only for the open browser tab, is forgotten when the tab closes, and is never saved to the site’s long-term storage.

If you link a RAPP Mirror, its address and a device key created during pairing, kept the same way as the secret. The key never leaves this device; the pairing code that created it is not stored.

Conversations and agent cards are kept in memory while the app is open. They are cleared when you start a new conversation, erase data, or close the app.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

When you speak, type, or choose a suggestion, the text and a short window of the current conversation are sent to the brainstem address you set, along with its secret if you saved one. The app also asks that brainstem for its status and reads its agent files to build capability-scanned cards on this device; agent code is never stored or shared.

If you link a RAPP Mirror, those requests — and what you ask the Mirror and its agents to do — go to that Mirror on your network, each signed with the device key, and the Mirror passes brainstem requests to its brainstem.

Your brainstem is software you run and control. It may send conversations to the AI model service it is configured to use, such as GitHub Copilot, under that service’s terms. Rapp Hive’s developer operates no server and receives none of this data.

A card’s sharing code carries only an agent’s recipe — its name, description, steps, and parameters — never code, and only when you show it to someone.

## Voice and camera

The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you are talking to the orb, after you grant access. When your device supports on-device recognition, audio stays on the device; otherwise your platform’s speech service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android, or your browser’s provider on the web) processes it under its own privacy policy. Only the resulting text is sent to your brainstem. Spoken replies use your device’s built-in voice.

The camera is used only on the Scan screen, to read a pairing code or an agent card. Frames are read on the device and never stored or sent.

## Sharing a card, and contacting us

Share card opens your device’s share sheet with a picture of the card, its name, verdict, and summary, a link to Rapp Hive on the App Store, and, for a recipe card, its rapp://agent link. They go only to the apps and people you choose, and Save Image adds the picture to your photos. The picture is made on this device and saved only as a temporary file for the share sheet; the app never reads your photo library.

Become a founder opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you send it. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them to reply, to keep track of founders and their perks, and to send the weekly founders email you asked for. Every weekly email has an unsubscribe link, you can ask us to delete your address at any time, and we never sell or share it. If you join the Founders Club on Discord, Discord’s own terms and privacy policy apply there.

## What the app does not do

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party services that receive your data. Nothing you scan or open installs an agent.

## Your control

Change the brainstem address, forget its secret, unlink a Mirror, turn spoken replies off, or erase all app data in Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Anything your brainstem keeps is managed on your brainstem.

Your device may include the app’s settings in iCloud or computer backups, according to your device settings. The secret is saved with this-device-only protection, so it does not sync through iCloud Keychain or move to a new device.

## Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at this policy’s web address with a new date.
