# Rapp Hive Privacy Policy

_Last updated September 28, 2026_

Rapp Hive does not collect personal data. Its brainstem runs on your iPhone, with Apple’s on-device model or, if you sign in, your own GitHub Copilot. Its developer never receives your conversations, voice, memories, agents, or settings.

## What the app stores on your device

Who answers on this iPhone (Apple’s model or GitHub Copilot, and which Copilot model) and whether replies are spoken aloud, in the app’s private on-device storage.

What you ask the brainstem on this iPhone to remember. Its memory agents save those notes in the app’s private storage, protected by your iPhone’s passcode, and read them back to answer you. With Apple’s on-device model answering, they never leave the device; with GitHub Copilot answering, recent memories go to GitHub Copilot with each question, as described below. Erase all data deletes them.

Your GitHub sign-in, only if you sign in for GitHub Copilot: the tokens GitHub issues are kept in the device keychain, limited to this device. Signing out, or erasing all data, deletes them.

Conversations and agent cards are kept in memory while the app is open. They are cleared when you start a new conversation, erase data, or close the app. Export transcript saves the conversation as a file that goes only where you send it, and Import transcript reads only the file you pick.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

When Apple’s on-device model answers on this iPhone, what you say or type is not sent anywhere.

If you choose GitHub Copilot for this iPhone, the brainstem on this iPhone sends what you say, a short window of the conversation, and its instructions (including the memories it recalls for that answer) to GitHub Copilot through your own GitHub account, under GitHub’s terms and privacy statement. Signing in happens on GitHub’s website, with a one-time code; the app never sees your GitHub password.

When you ask about Hacker News, the brainstem’s Hacker News agent fetches public stories from Hacker News’s API. It sends nothing about you.

A card’s sharing code carries only an agent’s recipe — its name, description, steps, and parameters — never code, and only when you show it to someone.

## Voice

The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you are talking to the orb, after you grant access. When your device supports on-device recognition, audio stays on the device; otherwise your platform’s speech service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android, or your browser’s provider on the web) processes it under its own privacy policy. Only the resulting text is sent to your brainstem. Spoken replies use your device’s built-in voice.

## Sharing a card, and contacting us

Share card opens your device’s share sheet with a picture of the card, its name, verdict, and summary, a link to Rapp Hive on the App Store, and, for a recipe card, its rapp://agent link. They go only to the apps and people you choose, and Save Image adds the picture to your photos. The picture is made on this device and saved only as a temporary file for the share sheet; the app never reads your photo library.

Become a founder opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you send it. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them to reply, to keep track of founders and their perks, and to send the weekly founders email you asked for. Every weekly email has an unsubscribe link, you can ask us to delete your address at any time, and we never sell or share it. If you join the Founders Club on Discord, Discord’s own terms and privacy policy apply there.

## What the app does not do

There are no Rapp Hive accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The only service that receives what you say is the one you choose: GitHub Copilot, if you sign in. Nothing you open installs an agent.

## Your control

Sign out of GitHub, turn spoken replies off, or erase all app data in Settings at any time. Erase all data also deletes the memories kept on this iPhone and signs you out of GitHub. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Your device may include the app’s settings and saved memories in iCloud or computer backups, according to your device settings. The GitHub sign-in is saved with this-device-only protection, so it does not sync through iCloud Keychain or move to a new device.

## Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at this policy’s web address with a new date.
